Featured Work

Design that changes how people live.

Two projects that show what climate-responsive, people-first architecture looks like in practice.

Enugu, Nigeria · Residential

A home that runs on nature 70% of the time.

A 7-bedroom family home designed to rely on natural ventilation and fans instead of constant AC. The result: a cooler, calmer home that costs a fraction of the energy to run.

  • Cross-ventilation orientation
  • Large, strategically placed windows
  • Atrium + high ceilings
  • Trees as thermal buffers
  • Deep shading & cantilevers
  • Light, breathable interiors

Casamance, Senegal · Community

Architecture as a tool for development.

A community centre designed for rural Casamance where poverty, education access and climate pressure intersect. The building is more than a building: it's a workshop, library, market, museum and gathering space in one.

  • Creative workshop
  • Library & study area
  • Craft shop + museum
  • Small market
  • Flexible community space
  • Local materials & climate logic
